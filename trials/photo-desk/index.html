drag to move

drag an object or select it and use arrow keys. r rotates, + / − resizes, f flips or switches. double-click to flip or switch.

a shared experiment with placeholder images. changes are public and saved for the next visitor. made with playhtml.

about this desk

object positions, rotation, size, and switches are stored by playhtml’s service and shared with everyone on this page. live cursors are temporary. playhtml also saves a random visitor identity in your browser. shared room data is public and unencrypted; keep personal information off the desk. if the connection drops, changes wait in this tab until it reconnects. all pictures here are drawn placeholders.
